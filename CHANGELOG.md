# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-15

First public release.

- Keen 1 style platforming: momentum, hold-to-jump-higher, pogo stick,
  plasma pistol with limited charges, one-hit deaths, extra life every 20,000.
- Eight levels across Doom's Episode 1, with tech, nukage and Hell tile sets,
  keycard doors and a two-Baron boss fight in E1M8.
- Phobos overworld with force fields that open as levels are cleared.
- Zombiemen, Imps, Pinkies, Lost Souls and Barons of Hell with Keen-style AI.
- Original synthesized soundtrack with a track per screen and a boss theme.
- Title, story, status screen, intermission tally, game over, ending and auto-save.
- Level reachability checker and 99 automated tests.
